# Parts list

Generated by `build_kicad.py` from the boards themselves, so it counts
what is actually placed. Every logic part is a DIP; 74HC throughout,
which is what the supply estimate in `BACKPLANE.md` assumes.

## Logic and memory

| Part | Package | Function | datapath | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `74HC153` | DIP-16 | dual 4:1 multiplexer | 8 | **8** |
| `74HC377` | DIP-20 | octal register, clock enable | 4 | **4** |
| `74HC157` | DIP-16 | quad 2:1 multiplexer | 2 | **2** |
| `74HC04` | DIP-14 | hex inverter | 1 | **1** |

15 chips in total. Sockets are worth it for the two memory types, which are the
parts you will want to reprogram. `74HC21` and `74HC27` are the two
that are thinly stocked; the LS or HCT versions drop in.
